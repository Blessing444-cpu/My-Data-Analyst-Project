Sales Trend & Profit Analysis Dashboard 
Project Overview: This project analyzes sales performanance, profit trend, and customer purchasing behaviour across different cities and product categories.
Tools Used : Power BI, Excel, Dax
Key Metrics: Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Total Orders
Key Insights: 1) Lagos generates the highest total sales, making it the strongest revenue market. 2) Electronics product drive the most revenue overall. 3) Beauty product show stronger profit margin compared to other categories. 4) Sales trend fluctuate, with noticeable peaks mid month.
